In an AI interview, Fabric's voice interviewer asks you about a process and follows up on your answers. It is the fastest way to share how work gets done. You need contributor or admin access.

## Before you start

You need a working microphone and a browser that can record audio, such as a current version of Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. Fabric must be opened over a secure (https) connection.

## Start an interview

1. Open the process you want to talk about.
2. In the process header, select **Start AI interview**. On a phone, tap the **+** button (Add a contribution) and choose it there.
3. Review your name, which comes from your Fabric profile.
4. Read the recording notice. It says the interview will be recorded, transcribed and stored to help document your organization's processes. The dialog also asks you to focus on the steps, tools and handoffs, and to avoid sensitive information such as salaries, personal situations or negative comments about individuals.
5. Select **Start interview** and allow microphone access when your browser asks.

The interviewer uses the process details and what is already captured about it to shape its questions.

## During the interview

Speak naturally. The screen shows "Listening…" while you talk and "Fabric is speaking…" while the interviewer answers. You can:

- Mute or unmute your microphone.
- Show a text box and type a message instead of speaking.
- Select **End interview** when you are done. Fabric asks you to confirm, and everything said so far is saved and processed.

## After you end it

Fabric shows "Processing your interview…". You can close the window. Processing carries on, and the interview appears on the process's Contributions tab when it is ready. See "What happens after you contribute" for the stages.

## If something goes wrong

- If the microphone is blocked, missing or busy, the dialog explains the fix for that cause. It also offers **Upload files instead**.
- If the connection drops, you can select **Try again**. If your interview was long enough, it may still have been captured, so check the Contributions tab after a minute.
- If an interview later shows as Failed, an admin can retry it.
